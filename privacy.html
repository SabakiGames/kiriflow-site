Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-12

Who uses this

KiriFlow is used only by the operator (@SabakiStream). It is not a public app, not a SaaS, and not a product that other people log into.

What we collect

We do not collect viewer information. We do not run ads. We do not sell data. Chat that appears in a clip is already on the original stream; KiriFlow does not build a viewer database.

Credentials

YouTube, Twitch, or similar tokens stay on the operator’s machine. They are not committed to git. API keys (including any transcription keys) are not published on this site or in the public repository.

Questions: @SabakiStream on X.
