What this is

KiriFlow is a personal editing workflow for unofficial fan clips. It is not a SaaS, not a marketplace, and not an official TikTok, YouTube, Twitch, or talent agency service.

Who may use it

Only the operator uses KiriFlow. There are no customer accounts.

Publishing

Nothing is published automatically. A human reviews every clip and uploads only after that review. Posts are unofficial fan clips, not official talent or platform statements.

Conduct

Clips must not be used to defame, harass, or misrepresent the people in them. Malicious cuts and outrage bait are not part of this workflow.
